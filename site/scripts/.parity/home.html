Why families choose us

A different kind of preschool

Most preschools ask you to drop off and trust the process. WCP asks you to be part of it.

  • Play-based learning

    Children learn best by doing. Our days are hands-on and curious, built on Ohio early learning standards.

  • A real co-op community

    You are not just dropping off. Parents help in the classroom and run the school together.

  • Experienced teachers

    Our lead teachers have taught here for years and genuinely know young children.

  • Genuinely affordable

    Co-op tuition is a fraction of typical preschool cost, starting at $70 a month.

  • Small class sizes

    Classes stay small, so every child gets real attention from a teacher who knows them.

  • Kindergarten ready

    Our Pre-K sends children on to kindergarten confident, curious, and ready to learn.

More than a preschool. A community that grows with you.

The co-op difference

You help run the school

Families are the school. Parents help in the classroom a couple of times a month, hold a co-op job for the year, and make decisions together. It is more work than a drop-off preschool, and it builds a community your child grows up inside.

How the co-op works

Front-Row Learning

Where play is the plan.

Play is the curriculum here, not a break from it. And because we are a co-op, you are in the room to watch it happen: children learning by doing, and you with a front-row seat to who they are becoming.

What does a typical day look like?

Predictable rhythm, lots of play. Every class follows the same gentle flow.

  1. 1

    9:30

    Greeting & free play

    Hang up your coat, choose a station, and ease into the morning.

  2. 2

    9:45

    Circle time

    Songs, the calendar, weather, and the day theme, all together on the rug.

  3. 3

    10:00

    Centers & activities

    Art, sensory bins, building, and small-group work tied to the week focus.

  4. 4

    10:50

    Snack

    A calm shared snack and a chance to practice pouring, sharing, and cleanup.

  5. 5

    11:40

    Outdoor play & dismissal

    Fresh air on the playground, then hugs goodbye at pickup.
