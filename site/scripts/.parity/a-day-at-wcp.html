A Peek Inside a WCP Morning

What a day at WCP actually looks like.

Every session at WCP is a mix of free play, guided activities, snack, and outdoor time. Here is a full look at how each class spends their morning, or afternoon.

Every Single Session

What all three classes have in common

No matter which class your child is in, every WCP session includes these five things.

  • Free Play

    Child-led exploration with toys, art, and open materials.

  • Circle Time

    Songs, stories, and group conversation on the rug.

  • Snack Time

    Parent helpers bring snack. Kids eat and talk together.

  • Parent Helpers

    Co-op parents help in the classroom every session, two in Twos and Threes, one in each Pre-K class.

  • Outdoor Play

    Every session ends with fresh air and playground time.

Follow along

How a morning unfolds

From the first hello to the last hug, here is the shape of a WCP session.

  1. Arrival and hellos

    Backpacks go in cubbies, coats on hooks, and a teacher who knows your child by name says good morning at the door.

  2. Free play and centers

    Blocks, easel paint, dramatic play, puzzles. Children choose where to start and teachers meet them there.

  3. Circle time

    Songs, the calendar, a story, and a look at what the day holds. Everyone has a spot on the rug.

  4. Snack together

    The helper parent serves snack family-style. Please and thank you get lots of practice.

  5. Outside time

    Our playground gets used in almost any weather. Climbing, digging, running, and fresh air every day we can.

  6. Story and goodbye

    A quiet wind-down, a recap of the day, and hugs at pickup. Ask your child about their favorite part.

Every class follows this rhythm. Exact times for each class are in the schedules below.
