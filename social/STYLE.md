# Social style guide

How WCP posts look and sound. Keep it consistent with the website (see
`site/PRODUCT.md` and the brand memory) so a reel feels like the same school.

## Look

- **Vertical 1080x1920, 30 fps.** Keep text inside the middle 1080x1420 or so:
  Instagram and Facebook cover the top ~220 px and bottom ~300 px with UI.
- **Scrapbook, not slick.** Photos as white-bordered prints with soft shadows,
  slightly rotated. Paper-textured solid backgrounds. Stickers (rounded white or
  amber labels) for captions. This matches the site's "Construction Paper"
  direction. Avoid glossy gradients, lens flares, stock transitions, and generic
  AI-looking motion graphics.
- **Font:** Captain Comic for everything. It renders as all caps.
- **Palette:**

  | Name | Hex | Use |
  |---|---|---|
  | Navy | `#01457E` | text on light, backgrounds for big statements, end card |
  | Sky | `#40AAED` | Pre-K PM background |
  | Orange | `#FF8C00` | Pre-K AM background, stamps |
  | Amber | `#FFA334` | Twos background, highlight stickers, numbers on navy |
  | Green | `#22C55E` | Threes background |
  | Cream | `#FFF6E6` | neutral montage background |

  Use the class color as the main background so each class has its own look
  (Twos amber, Threes green, Pre-K AM orange, Pre-K PM sky, Summer navy).
  Navy or white text on bright colors; amber or white text on navy.
- **End card:** navy paper, white WCP logo, a warm one-line sign-off in amber,
  "Love, <class>" in white, confetti optional.

## Voice

- Warm, a little dry, never mocking the kids. The joke is the universal
  preschool truth (99 pumps of soap, the snack negotiations), and the kids
  are always the heroes.
- On-screen text is short: 2 to 6 words per sticker, one idea per beat.
- Parent-facing, not marketing-speak. Write like a class parent, not an ad.
- No em-dashes anywhere.

## Structure that works

1. **Hook (0 to 2 s):** a title card over the real footage ("Hand-washing 101
   (harder than it looks)").
2. **Setup:** real dialogue as captions; a freeze-frame on the question.
3. **Payoff:** one big slammed word or number, with a shake and a hit sound.
4. **Proof / gag:** photos that land the joke, a zoom to a funny detail.
5. **Montage:** "and this week we also..." cards with check stickers.
6. **End card:** logo and sign-off.

Sound: real classroom audio first, synthesized pops/dings/whooshes on every
sticker, a drumroll into the payoff, music entering *after* the dialogue.

## Captions

Template:

> <Class>'s big lesson this week: <the thing>. <emoji>
> <The punchline, short.> <emoji> (<a wink>.)
> <Other activities in a list>. <emoji>
> #WestChesterPreschool #<ClassTag> #CoopPreschool #WestChesterOhio #PreschoolLife

- West Chester, **Ohio** (not PA).
- 2 to 3 emoji total, 4 to 6 hashtags.
- No children's names. Teachers by name only if Nathan says it's fine.
